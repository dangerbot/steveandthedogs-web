The Vision

The magic is the bond between a team and its fans.

It is one of the most powerful relationships there is, and it is bigger than a ticket. A fan gives a team their weekends, their loyalty, their kids' first games. That bond is the whole business. Everything else flows from it.

Today that relationship is delivered in pieces. A website owns one moment, a ticketing vendor another, an app another, each seeing only a sliver of the fan. The connection gets handed off and dropped between a dozen tools that never talk, and the magic leaks through the seams.

Envorso Sports makes the whole relationship one thing. One platform across every moment, so the fan is never handed off and the magic never leaks. Eight modules. One fan profile. Zero integrations.

The Principle

We do what we do best, so the team can do what they do best.

The front office already lives and breathes the fan, selling the seats, building the relationship, making game day great. That is their craft. They should not have to become technologists to do it well.

Our job is the tools that make their job easier and more powerful. One platform instead of many, with AI doing the heavy lifting, so their energy stays on the fan, not on wrangling software.

And we only win when they do. We earn a share of the revenue we help create, not a license fee.

The Strategy

Raise the value of the game.

Revenue per game is the number owners feel, and it grows one way that lasts: make the game worth more, and grow the demand for it. Not by racing to the bottom on price, and not by pricing the everyday fan out. A fan who values the night comes back, brings a friend, and spends around the game. The experience is the engine. Revenue is what it throws off.

Better experience→ More value per fan→ More revenue per game→ reinvest ↻

Reinvest in the experience, and the loop compounds. Every game raises the value again.

Where we win
The Seawolves first, because we own the team to prove it on. Then the rest of Major League Rugby. Then the underserved sports the big leagues left behind, like cricket and lacrosse, with the US rugby World Cup on the horizon.
How we win
Raise the value of the game so revenue compounds, and run it all on one platform that sees the whole fan. That unified view is the moat a single-feature vendor cannot copy.
What has to be true
The experience comes first, because without it there is nothing to monetize. Onboarding the next team has to be near-effortless. And the partnership has to stay aligned, where we win when the team wins.
The Moat

The moat is the mix.

Every fan signal flows through one platform. Tickets, picks, credits, merch, attendance, and every nudge a fan responded to or ignored.

A standalone ticketing vendor sees tickets. A standalone app sees sessions. A standalone CRM sees campaigns. None of them sees the whole fan. We do.

The more games we run, the more we learn. The more we learn, the sharper the nudges. The platform compounds. A single-feature vendor cannot.

The Growth Unlock

Make the next team's onboarding easier than the last.

The Seawolves are not just our first customer. They are where we perfect the cutover and the ramp, so every team after them is faster and cleaner to bring on.

The hard part of adoption was never the demo. It is the switch. Zero integrations removes the integration tax, but every team still arrives with years of history in disparate systems. Season-ticket records in one tool, email lists in another, merch and CRM and ticketing somewhere else. In my experience, that migration is the moment deals stall. How do I get everything I already have into the new system, fast, without a six-month project?

STEP 01

Ingest

AI-assisted import pulls a team's existing data and knowledge out of its scattered tools.

STEP 02

Seed

That history seeds the unified fan profile automatically. No blank slate, no manual rebuild.

STEP 03

Value on day one

The front office feels the benefit immediately, not after a quarter of setup.

Win the owner

With the value. More revenue per game, a fan base that comes back, one system instead of many.

Win the front office

With how easy it is to switch on, and how easy it is to use. They live in it every day. Make it lighter than what they had, and they become the advocates who sell the next team for us.

Growth runs two ways. Every new team onboards faster than the last, because we learn with each one. And every team already on the platform is one we keep adding to, seamlessly, as the product grows. Easier to implement. Easier to use. More powerful every release.

My Throughline

I have spent my career on one question.

How do you get someone to come back tomorrow? Not once. As a habit. That means finding the moments that matter, designing for clarity, and building experiences people trust.

I learned the craft at Adidas, Nike, and Bowflex, and sharpened it in startups. The fundamentals never change: understand the user, get the product right, earn the next visit. What has changed is the speed. AI lets me push those fundamentals further and faster than ever.

That is what pulls me to Envorso Sports. The same fundamentals, the same obsession with the user, now in service of the fan. I look forward to pushing the boundaries of what we can do, in ultimate service of the user.
