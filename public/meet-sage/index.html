Meet Sage.

A clickable concept exploring what AI-led onboarding could feel like for a connected-hardware app. Built with Claude in a couple of afternoons.

Why this exists.

Onboarding is the most neglected screen in most apps. It's the front door, the first thing a new user touches, and most teams treat it like a form to fill out instead of a moment to win.

This concept reimagines what onboarding could look like if you led with the AI character your users will spend their time with, not with another password screen.

What we tested.

A quick comparison study with 24 new Bloomers. Half walked the original flow. Half walked this one.
